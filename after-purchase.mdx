Checkout is handled by Tebex and licensing is tied to your Cfx.re account. After you pay, you do not receive a zip in email. You download the resource from the **Cfx.re Portal**.

<Info>
  Free resources follow the same path. Claim them from the [store](https://bundlyscripts.com/store), then download them from Granted Assets like any other purchase.
</Info>

## What happens after checkout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tebex sends your receipt" icon="envelope">
    Tebex emails a receipt to the address you used at checkout. Billing and refunds are handled by Tebex.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The resource is granted to your Cfx.re account" icon="key">
    Delivery goes to the Cfx.re account you logged in with on [bundlyscripts.com](https://bundlyscripts.com) before paying. That is the account that owns the asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discord roles are assigned" icon="discord" iconType="brands">
    If you linked Discord in the cart before checkout, customer roles are assigned automatically. You can still join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/CnQcfsw9G) later for support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Delivery can take a minute or two after payment clears. If Granted Assets is empty, wait briefly, then refresh.
</Note>

## Download from the Cfx.re Portal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Granted Assets">
    Sign in to the Cfx.re Portal with the **same** FiveM account you used at checkout, then open [Granted Assets](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets).

    [Open Cfx.re Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Bundly resource">
    Your purchases and free claims appear in the list. Select the resource you just bought.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the latest version">
    Download the package and extract it. Keep the folder name as it shipped, for example `bundly-loadingscreen`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it into your server">
    Copy the folder into your server `resources` directory. A dedicated folder such as `resources/[bundlyscripts]` keeps Bundly resources together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure it in server.cfg">
    Add the resource to `server.cfg` and restart, or start it live:

    ```cfg
    ensure bundly-loadingscreen
    ```

    If you keep Bundly resources in a folder, you can start the whole folder:

    ```cfg
    ensure [bundlyscripts]
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The purchase is bound to the Cfx.re account used at checkout. If you logged into the store with a different account than the one that runs the server, the asset will not appear on the server account.
</Warning>

## Updates

Updates are published through the Cfx.re Portal, not as a separate download link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the new version">
    Open [Granted Assets](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets) and download the latest build of the resource.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the resource folder">
    Stop the resource, replace the old folder with the new one, then start it again. Do not merge two versions on top of each other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your config">
    Copy your edited `config` file back in after you replace the folder, then check the changelog or docs for any new options.
  </Step>
</Steps>
